what am I looking at?
  • host: the name of the server your computer contacted (a dash means no name was found).
  • ip: that server's address on the internet.
  • port: which "door" on the server was used. 443 is secure web traffic, 53 is DNS.
  • program: the app on your computer that made the connection (blank if it could not be matched).
  • country / owner: where the IP is registered and which company owns it. LAN means it is on your own network.
  • packets: how many chunks of data were sent. Bigger means busier.
  • Amber rows use an uncommon port. That is only a hint to look closer, not proof of anything bad.
  • baseline mode: press learn and use your computer normally for a few minutes. Then press watch. Anything new or unusual compared with what it learned shows up under alerts.
